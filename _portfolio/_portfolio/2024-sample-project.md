This project developed semiconductor topological quantum photonic integrated circuits for the generation, transfer, and manipulation of light at the single-photon limit on-chip. The work was recognized with the UK's Rank Prize Award in 2021.

## Key contributions

- Designed and demonstrated chiral and topological photonic circuit architectures for integrated quantum optics
- Established robust on-chip routing of single photons emitted by embedded semiconductor quantum dots
- Pioneered chiral topological add-drop filtering for quantum photonic circuits
