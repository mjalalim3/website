Mahmoud Jalali Mehrabad

Research Scientist | MIT

Mahmoud Jalali Mehrabad is a Research Scientist at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), where he is part of the Quantum Photonic & AI Group. His research develops integrated photonic devices that bridge fundamental physics with scalable technologies for nonlinear photonics, quantum systems, and AI-augmented hardware.

He received his PhD in quantum optics as a University Prize Scholarship Fellow from the University of Sheffield, UK, in 2021, under the supervision of Profs. Maurice Skolnick (FRS), Mark Fox, and Luke Wilson, where his work on semiconductor topological quantum photonic integrated circuits was recognized with the UK's Rank Prize Award. He subsequently joined the Joint Quantum Institute at the University of Maryland (2022–2025) as a postdoctoral researcher in Prof. Mohammad Hafezi's lab, before continuing his research at MIT.

His current research focuses on multiscale nonlinear photonic devices, topological and chiral photonics, quantum optoelectronics in 2D materials, and AI-augmented photonic design.
